# Queued — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 23 October 2026_

Queued is a message-scheduling app for Android. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

## Summary

Queued keeps everything on your phone. There is no Queued server, no account, no analytics and no ads. The developer never receives your messages, contacts or any other data.

## Data the app uses

| Data | Why | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| Messages you write, recipients, schedules, templates, birthdays, saved groups | To send or remind you at the right time | Stored only on your phone |
| Contacts (only if you allow it) | Birthday autopilot reads contact names, birthdays and phone numbers to set up wishes | Stored only on your phone |
| Send history | Shows Reports and suggests a usual time to message someone | Stored only on your phone; you can export it as a CSV file yourself |
| Telegram bot token (only if you add one) | To post to your Telegram groups and channels | Stored on your phone; sent only to Telegram's servers (api.telegram.org) when a message is posted |
| Files you import (CSV / Excel) | To create one message per row | Read on your phone; not uploaded anywhere |

## Messages sent through other apps

- **Telegram groups and channels:** the message text is sent to Telegram through your own bot. Telegram's privacy policy applies to that message.
- **WhatsApp, SMS, Instagram, Telegram chats and Email:** Queued opens that app with your message ready, and you send it yourself. That app's privacy policy applies.

## On-device AI and translation

AI rewrite (Google ML Kit with Gemini Nano) and translation (Google ML Kit) run entirely on your phone. Your text is not sent to Google or anyone else. Google Play services may download the language or AI models once.

## Sharing

Queued does not sell or share your data with anyone.

## Deleting your data

Delete messages, birthdays or templates inside the app, or uninstall the app to remove everything it stored.

## Children

Queued is not directed at children under 13.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at the developer's GitHub, or email the developer through the contact listed on the Google Play page.
